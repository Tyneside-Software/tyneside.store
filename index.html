This is a sketch, not an active Tyneside shop. A friend runs RST Wholesale (rst-wholesale.com) — craft blanks, drinkware, stationery, clothing. We mirror their public catalogue so it can wear a Tyneside face. Every collection and product still opens on RST. They take the order. They ship. We do not hold stock and we do not take payment.

Honest status

No Tyneside checkout. Clicking through to RST means you pay RST’s price, not a Tyneside markup. The +2% to tyneside.charity only works if we ever take the order ourselves — and that charity is not registered yet, so no Gift Aid either. Until then this page is a signpost to a friend’s wholesale shop.

3. Later: +2% to charity

If this door ever takes payment, the idea is RST price + 2%, and that 2% to tyneside.charity (Howden welcome-home cleans). Example: RST £10.00 → £10.20 here (£0.20 charity). Same pattern as tyneside.beer (Dynamite Valley dropship). That is not what happens on a click-through today.
